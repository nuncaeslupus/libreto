Chapter 1

Rain had been falling on the Blue Lamp since noon, and by six the gutters on Harborstreet had given up. Vega sat at the window table with her coat still on, watching the door the way other people watch a kettle.

She had received no word since half past, and it was a quarter to seven now. The only person who had come in was a boy with wet hair and a green ledger under his arm, who looked at her, looked away, and then walked straight to her table as if someone had sent him. He put two envelopes down in front of her and left without a word.

On the table between them the leters was still sealed, and she did not touch them.

Not here, not with the whole bar watching.
