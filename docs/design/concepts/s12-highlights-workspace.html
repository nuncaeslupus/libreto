“Then hear this.” Lena leaned close enough that Vega could smell the lemon on her hands. “Go home. Don’t come back to the Blue Lamp tomorrow. And don’t ask Kit what he saw.”

She did not open the envelope until she was home, with the door locked and the chain on. Inside was a single sheet from Kit’s ledger, torn along the stitching.

Block quote

Vega, don’t come looking. The ledger is safe where it is, and so am I.

Burn this. K.

She did not burn it. She read it four times, the way she had once read a paperback about Harbor Street, and then she put it in the drawer with the others.

It was a good lie. Vega had heard worse from people who were paid to tell them. But there was a wet ring on the bar by the back-room door, and a second glass upside down on the drainer, still beaded.
