CHAPTER 9

The pawnshop

The pawnshop had been a chemist’s once, and still smelled of it. Behind the counter a man with a jeweller’s glass in one eye was pretending not to have seen her come in.

Vega waited. It was one of the few things she was good at that nobody ever paid her for.
