CHAPTER 7

Lena’s warning

The Blue Lamp was nearly empty by ten, which suited Vega. The jukebox was off, and the only sound was Lena drying glasses that were already dry.

“You’re early,” Lena said. “Or late.”

Vega sat at the end of the bar, near the door to the back room. “Has Kit been in?”

“Kit who?” Lena set a glass down hard. “Half the city’s called Kit.”

“Mine. Thin, nervous, writes everything down in a little green ledger. He said he’d meet me here at nine.”

Lena looked at the door behind her, then at nothing at all. “Nobody came in at nine. Nobody’s been in that room since Monday.”
