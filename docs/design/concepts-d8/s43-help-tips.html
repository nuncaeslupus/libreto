CHAPTER 7

Lena’s warning

Lena set a glass down hard enough to make it ring. “Half the city’s called Kit,” she said, and went on drying.

Vega took the stool at the end of the bar, the one with a view of the back-room door. “Has Kit been in?”

“Nobody came in at nine,” Lena said. “Nobody’s been in that room since Tuesday.”

It was a good lie. Vega had heard worse from people who were paid to tell them.

Show: what the text wears

You pressed F1 with “Show” focused in the status bar

Open Show ▾ at the right of the status bar to choose what you see while you write: the marks, whose notes (mine, all or none), scene names, and whether the text is the whole book or one chapter.

Show names and styles (Ctrl+Shift+A) tints people, places and things and marks styled words. It changes only what you see, never the book.
