Pros and cons, against draft 8
The owner chooses. This cut is drawn only so the two can be put side by side.

What four modes gives

  • Keeps the idea of dressing the window for a job (write, plan, know, publish).
  • Four labels fit narrow windows and Spanish better than six.
  • Less redraw: most draft 7 screens stay as they were.

What it costs

  • Left and right columns change meaning per mode; Back must still cross modes.
  • A mode switch and a Navigator that changes: two things to learn, not one.
  • Codex and Research merge into a long list; the Inbox gets buried under the Codex.
  • Opening a Codex entry while writing changes mode, so the text leaves the screen; in draft 8 it opens beside it.
  • Book mixes the page preview and its settings in one place.
S50The alternative: four modes. Not the proposal: the other way to cut the app, drawn beside the draft 8 model so the owner can choose. Same chapter, same window width; the Notebook frame is drawn taller so its whole list shows.
