The ways back
Esc

Closes the innermost thing

A menu, a card, the quick look, a sheet, a dialog: the one in front, then the next. In the text it clears a selection and nothing else.

Alt+← Alt+→

Back and Forward

Walk every jump between documents: a chapter, a Codex entry, a topic. Long-press ‹ for the list. They never undo an edit.

Ctrl+Z

Undo the last thing here

Linear and local: the manuscript is one stack; each entry, topic and field has its own. It never takes you somewhere else.

Revert takes back one older thing

A review round, an agent session, a Board move after typing: a new step that undoes just that, and says what it could not. Edit ▸ Revert, or a notice once later steps exist.

On screen: the window with its menu bar, strip and status bar; the three columns (Navigator with Book · Codex · Research, the open document in Text · Board · Pages or an entry, topic or Brief, the side panel with Reference · Review · Ask conversation · Book layout); what opens over it (small cards, sheets, dialogs, windows); the four ways back  ·  Not on screen: modes, tabs, a bell, floating panels, a second window for the same project
